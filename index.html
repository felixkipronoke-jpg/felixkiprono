About

Curious about patterns, careful with evidence, driven by public access.

I love looking at data, spotting trends, and making meaningful connections. My work is about turning those connections into useful public knowledge, then using that knowledge to support innovation and solve real problems.

My long-term aim is to help collect publicly available datasets and make them easier for anyone to use, so more people can understand the world around them. I began in finance and accounting, a path that opened the door to data, systems, controls, and the discipline of asking better questions.
